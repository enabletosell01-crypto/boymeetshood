COMING SOON NFT LENDING · BNPL

Borrow against your NFT without selling it. Hood Credit introduces an NFT lending marketplace where eligible NFTs can be used as collateral.

P2P Lending

Lenders create offers specifying:

· Loan amount
· Interest rate
· Duration
· Accepted collateral

Borrowers choose the offer that fits them.

Pool Lending

Liquidity providers deposit capital into lending pools. The protocol determines borrowing limits using predefined risk parameters.

Maximum Loan = NFT Value × LTV
NFT value1 ETH
Maximum LTV40%
Maximum loan0.40 ETH
Default

The NFT remains locked in smart-contract escrow until repayment. If the borrower doesn't repay within the agreed period, the protocol can liquidate the collateral according to predefined smart-contract rules.

Lenders receive the applicable recovery proceeds. Borrowers keep their NFT if they repay.

Simple.
Buy Now, Pay Later

Why should buying an NFT require having the entire bag immediately? Hood Credit can support NFT BNPL-style financing for eligible marketplace purchases.

NFT price1 ETH
Buyer contributes0.30 ETH
Protocol financing0.70 ETH

The NFT is placed into the appropriate escrow structure until the financing is repaid. Everyone knows the rules before the transaction.
