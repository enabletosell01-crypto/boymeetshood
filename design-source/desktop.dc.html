Hood Credit

COMING SOON

Borrow against your NFT without selling it. HoodMeetsBoy introduces an NFT lending marketplace where eligible NFTs can be used as collateral.

P2P Lending

Lenders create offers specifying:

· Loan amount
· Interest rate
· Duration
· Accepted collateral

Borrowers choose the offer that fits them.

Pool Lending

Liquidity providers can deposit capital into lending pools. The protocol determines borrowing limits using predefined risk parameters such as:

Maximum Loan = NFT Value × LTV
NFT value = 1 ETH
Maximum LTV = 40%
Maximum loan = 0.40 ETH

Default

The NFT remains locked in smart-contract escrow until repayment. If the borrower doesn't repay within the agreed period, the protocol can liquidate the collateral according to predefined smart-contract rules.

Lenders receive the applicable recovery proceeds. Borrowers keep their NFT if they repay.

Simple.
